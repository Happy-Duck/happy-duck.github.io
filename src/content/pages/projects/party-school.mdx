## What it is

Party School is a third-person multiplayer party game set on the University of Illinois campus, in a low-poly style. Players run around campus with their friends, wait in a lobby, and walk to wherever the next minigame is being played. Between rounds the campus itself is the game: there are collectibles hidden around it to find.

It was the final project for [CS 415, Game Development](/courses/cs-415/), built by a team of five for PC. The team wrote it for three kinds of player, in this order: UIUC students who play games, people who like an immersive world, and casual multiplayer players.

<Note title="Where it came from">
The design document lists what each idea was borrowed from: Fall Guys for parkour party games, Mario Party for the minigame format, Slender for avoid-and-collect horror, Garry's Mod for minigames inside one fixed map, and Minecraft server lobbies for the open-world lobby.
</Note>

## The minigames

| Minigame | What the player does |
| --- | --- |
| Horror | Avoid and collect, in the style of Slender, with the Alma Mater statue doing the chasing. |
| Squirrel game | Find and photograph campus squirrels, which hide when a player gets close. |
| Prop hunt | Shoot friends who are disguised as props. |
| Open-world exploration | Explore the campus for collectibles, on foot or flying. |
| Racing | Drive cars around obstacles, with power-ups. A stretch goal in the plan. |

The design document says what each one should feel like: curious while exploring, surprised on finding a collectible, startled on spotting the Alma Mater in the horror game, and fast in the race.

## My part

I built the shared project base that every minigame sits on, including the multiplayer integration, and reviewed teammates' code as it came in. I brought the real campus into the engine by streaming a city-scale map from Google's Map Tiles API through the BLOSM plugin. I am also building the racing minigame on Unreal's Chaos Vehicle system.

## How the work was planned

The technical design document breaks the game into eight systems and gives every feature a priority, a difficulty, and a risk. This is its top level, as of version 1.2 (March 2026).

| System | What it covers | Top priority in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player | Walking, jumping, flying, driving, health, interacting, collecting | Walk, run, and jump |
| Shooting | Guns (hitscan), scopes, and the camera for the squirrel game | Firing a gun |
| Multiplayer | Lobbies, player names, join messages | Lobby handling |
| AI movement | The Alma Mater chasing players; squirrels hiding from them | Chasing the player |
| UI | Pause, lobby, and start menus; a scrapbook of achievements | The three menus |
| Map | Collectibles and a minimap | The minimap |
| Save system | Collectibles and achievements kept between sessions | Collectibles |
| Racing | Cars, obstacles, driving controls, start and finish, power-ups | All marked as stretch |

Behind those sit the systems a player never sees: navigation meshes for the AI, multiplayer networking, the mapping from the real world to 3D, and saving.
