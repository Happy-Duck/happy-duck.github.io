## The studio

Origami Games is a game studio based in Maharashtra, known for mobile horror games such as Floors of Fear. I spent June and July of 2023 there building prototypes for games the studio might make next, in Unity and C#.

## Endless runner

The player runs forward along a path without stopping and steers left and right by dragging, in the manner of Subway Surfers or Temple Run.

<Figure label="The endless runner: a figure running down a lane between obstacles" caption="Placeholder frame. There is a screenshot of this in the final presentation." />

The steering was the new part for me. I had never built this kind of input before.

## Procedural maze escape

The maze is not laid out by hand. An algorithm generates it, and the game builds what it comes up with as objects in the scene for the player to find a way out of.

<Figure label="The maze from above, with the player in a corridor" caption="Placeholder frame. There is a screenshot of this in the final presentation." />

The algorithm was the hard part. It took a good deal of research into maze generation before there was anything to turn into a playable level.

## What I took from it

- **Unity and C#**, used every day on something with a deadline.
- **How a studio works**: constant contact with a mentor, and how a game goes from an idea to something produced.
- **Iteration**: both prototypes were changed after several rounds of feedback, and ideas went back and forth with peers.
