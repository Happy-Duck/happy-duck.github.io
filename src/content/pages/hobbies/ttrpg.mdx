## Duckman

My first character, and the one who would not stay dead.

The start of his story never changed. He was left in a cave in the forest as a child, grew up with no one to teach him what his powers were, and learned to speak from a settlement of dwarves he found at seventeen. Everything after that is what the dice did to him.

The treasure box on his last sheet reads:

> Duckman has lost everything he once owned. His quarter staff, bag of a hundred darts, even his beloved rubber duck through which he channeled his sorcerous powers, all now burnt to ashes.

## Glorb

Made for a one-shot with friends. A plasmoid bard, three feet tall and translucent cyan, who can squeeze through a gap an inch wide.

- **Personality:** "Sleek and slippery both figuratively and metaphorically, could sell you your own dagger."
- **Ideal:** "A world with less order, and more freedom."
- **Flaw:** "Lonely in his heart, but nobody needs to know."
